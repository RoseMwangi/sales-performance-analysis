Strategic Performance Analysis for a Retail Business
Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive, data-driven analysis of a retail business's performance across its network of stores in India. The goal of this analysis was to uncover specific drivers of profitability, customer behavior, and operational efficiency. The final output is an actionable dashboard and key strategic recommendations for company leadership.

The Business Problem
The business sought to drive profitable growth but lacked a comprehensive understanding of its performance across key business functions. This lack of insight made it difficult to optimize our sales strategy, identify high-value customer segments, and replicate the success of top-performing branches.

This analysis was conducted to address these challenges and provide clear recommendations for improving profitability and efficiency across the organization.

Data & Methodology
Data Source: A synthetic, real-world-style dataset of Point-of-Sale (POS) logs from a retail business, consisting of 8 distinct tables: Customer demographic table, Inventory table, Logistics data table, Monthly seasonal planning table, product information table, promotions and discounts table, Sales date table, and Site details table.

Data Stack:

SQL: The dataset was first loaded into a Google BigQuery data warehouse. All data modeling, cleaning, and aggregation were performed using SQL queries in BigQuery. This approach allowed for efficient processing of large-scale data and demonstrates a real-world data workflow.

Dashboarding: The cleaned and aggregated data was connected to Looker Studio, where it was used to create interactive dashboards for key stakeholders.

Analysis: Insights were derived by joining multiple tables and using advanced SQL functions. The analysis focused on three key areas: profitability, customer behavior, and site performance.

Key Insights & Recommendations
Profitability Analysis: We identified a significant negative net profit of -€265,490, primarily driven by high returns and a high shrinkage rate. Recommendation: Launch an immediate investigation into the causes of these losses, particularly in the Apparel and Dairy categories.

Customer Insights: Our analysis revealed that a majority of our customers are male, indicating a large, untapped market of female clients. Recommendation: Launch targeted marketing campaigns to attract and retain female clients.

Site Performance: We were able to identify top-performing sites and key factors that contribute to their success. Recommendation: Replicate the successful strategies from our top-performing branches to improve the performance of underperforming locations.

Skills Demonstrated

Data Warehousing: Experience with cloud-based data warehouses like Google BigQuery.

Data Visualization & Storytelling: Created a comprehensive dashboard in Looker Studio to communicate findings to stakeholders in a clear, actionable format. This included using data blending, creating calculated fields, and implementing interactive filters.
